About Us

About Never Left Behind

Founded by Richard Sturd, Never Left Behind, Even When Your Home Inc. is a faith-based nonprofit committed to serving veterans who face challenges of homelessness, addiction, or life transition.

Our mission is simple: no veteran should ever be left behind. Through housing, recovery programs, skills training, and community reintegration, we help veterans discover purpose and hope again.

As a veteran-led team, we understand the struggles of service members. That’s why we stand shoulder-to-shoulder with our brothers and sisters, offering them the support, faith, and opportunities they deserve.

Richard Sturd Founder
