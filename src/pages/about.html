About Us

About Never Left Behind, Even When Your Home Inc.

Never Left Behind, Even When Your Home Inc. is a veteran support and rehabilitation mission built on faith and compassion. Founded by Ken Young, also known as "The Vessel," this organization exists to honor God and help U.S. veterans rebuild their lives after service by offering lodging, rehabilitation, training, and spiritual growth opportunities.

Our purpose is not just to provide services, but to walk alongside veterans as they overcome pain, find healing, and discover new paths forward. Through God's guidance, we create a safe place for soldiers to recover, grow, and transition successfully back into society.

I am Ken Young, the vessel chosen by God to begin this mission. This is not about me—it is about God's glory and His great love for those who sacrificed for others. My role is simply to obey, to listen, and to build what He has commanded: a place where no soldier is ever left behind.

Ken Young Founder, Never Left Behind (The Vessel)
